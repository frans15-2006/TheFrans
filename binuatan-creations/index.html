About

Rooted in the weave

Binuatan Creations is a handloom weaving and indigenous craft shop in Puerto Princesa City, Palawan. The workshop is known for turning locally sourced fibers and grasses into functional souvenirs and home goods that carry the look of Palawan craft.

The name Binuatan comes from the Cuyonon word for creation, a fitting match for a place where weaving, patience, and everyday utility sit side by side. Visitors often come for the finished pieces and stay for the chance to see how the loom works in person.

Real materials. Palawan's indigenous fibers and grasses are at the center of the shop's work.

Real use. The products are made to be carried, gifted, and used long after the trip ends.

Real place. The shop sits in Sta. Monica, Puerto Princesa City, where weaving remains part of the local story.
