Story

A quiet second-floor table with coffee and bowls.

Public reviews describe OurTable Coffee Shop as clean, comfortable, and friendly, with a menu that moves between coffee drinks, bagels, salads, bowls, and bulgogi plates. Tripadvisor traveler photos name items like OUR Signature Latte (Coconut Cream), Cheese Bulgogi Panini, Breakfast Yogurt Bowl, and the salad bowls, while other public listings mention grilled tofu bowls and sesame noodles. The cafe sits upstairs at Goland Plaza on Malvar Road in Puerto Princesa.

That mix of soft lighting, easy seating, and hearty plates gives the place its own pace: not rushed, not overly styled, just a solid table for coffee or lunch.
