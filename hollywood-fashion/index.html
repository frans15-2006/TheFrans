About the store

Built from perfume, expanded through style

The story started in 2009 with 13 bottles of imported perfume. The first store opened on February 13, 2010, and the main branch now serves Puerto Princesa from Rizal Avenue in Barangay San Miguel.

Hollywood Fashion Merchandise grew into a one-stop shop for imported and authentic products, with a mix that includes clothing, shoes, bags, accessories, perfumes, make-up, and fine jewelry. The brand's official focus is simple: authenticity, affordability, discounts, freebies, and customer service that keeps the experience easy.

The Fine Collection adds natural diamond jewelry, custom design work, and repair services such as stone replacement, ring sizing, chain repair, diamond setting, engraving, polishing, and free cleaning and inspection.
