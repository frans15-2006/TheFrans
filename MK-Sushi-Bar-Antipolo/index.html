Our Story

Where East
Meets Taste

MK Sushi Bar brings together the finest traditions of Japanese, Korean, and Asian fusion cuisine in a setting designed for those who appreciate both flavor and atmosphere. Located in Brgy. Dalig, Antipolo City — we've built a loyal community of 9,200+ followers who keep coming back.

Step into our fully air-conditioned dining space or settle into one of our authentic Japanese Tatami tables — a rare dining experience in Antipolo. Every dish is crafted with precision, every bowl of ramen is simmered with patience.

Available on FoodPanda for delivery, in-store pickup, and dine-in. We bring the experience to you, wherever you are.
