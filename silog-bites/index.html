The Story
Behind the Bite

SILOG BITES by Urban Eats was born out of a simple mission — serve hot, hearty Filipino comfort food to everyone in Mandaue Maguikay, Cebu and beyond.

From our d best empanada — golden, crispy, and always packed — to our classic tapsilogan meals, every dish is made with care and delivered with pride.

Part of the Urban Eats family — delivering comfort cuisine right to your door via GrabFood and Mubu.
