Amenities

Comfort that stays close to nature

Every feature here follows the same idea: keep the experience honest, quiet, and connected to the shoreline rather than overbuilt.

Solar and off-grid The property is powered entirely by solar energy, with a peaceful pace that supports a proper digital detox. Wi-Fi and air-conditioning are not available.
Dining A beachside restaurant serves freshly prepared meals with locally sourced ingredients, organic produce from the farm, fresh seafood, craft beer, and island coffee.
Wellness and firelight Guests can book a massage, gather for a beach BBQ, or end the day around a bonfire under the stars.
Nature-led stays Snorkeling, nature walks, island runs, and coconut-oil making are part of the resort's quiet island rhythm.
Practical comforts Free private parking is available on site, and the rooms include private bathrooms, clean linens, terraces, and sea views.
