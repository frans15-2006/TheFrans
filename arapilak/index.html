About

Low-waste living, shaped in Palawan.

AraPilak takes its name from the Cuyonon words ara and pilak, meaning no waste. Founded by Jude Rivera in 2018, the brand grew in Puerto Princesa around a simple idea: make sustainable choices easier to reach.

The shop began with reusable, low-waste goods and expanded into Ba'law, a clothing line that brings flour sacks back into everyday life through modern, handcrafted pieces.

Offerings

Useful pieces with a lighter footprint.

AraPilak offers practical alternatives for everyday routines, plus clothing and home goods that reuse what already has value.

Zero-waste essentials

Bamboo straws, stainless straws, bamboo utensils, tumblers, menstrual cups, and other low-waste basics designed to replace single-use items.

Ba'law clothing

Handcrafted tops, shorts, skirts, and dresses made from katsa or flour sacks, carrying the meaning of awareness in Cuyonon.

Repurposed home goods

Practical pieces such as repurposed coconut planters, created to keep materials in use and support a slower, more thoughtful kind of making.
