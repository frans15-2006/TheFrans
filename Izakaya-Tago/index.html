Our Story

The Spirit of
Izakaya

An izakaya is more than a restaurant. It is a place to slow down — where food and drink arrive at their own pace, where conversation fills the room, and where every dish tells a story rooted in Japanese tradition.

Izakaya Tago brings that spirit to Lipa City, Batangas. Located at G/F Hoogah 2021 Bldg along J.P. Laurel Highway, we serve a curated spread of ramen, yakiniku, hotpot, steak, sushi, and sashimi — all crafted with care.

With 11,000 followers and a 96% recommendation rate from 16 verified reviews, Tago has earned its place as Lipa City's Japanese dining destination.
