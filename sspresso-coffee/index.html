Our Story

BREWED WITH
INTENTION

SSpress O Coffee and Co was born from a simple belief — that great coffee should be accessible to everyone, even on an island paradise. Nestled inside Silver Shoppers at ERC Plaza in Barangay San Pedro, Puerto Princesa, we bring specialty-grade espresso to Palawan.

Every bean is carefully selected, every shot pulled with precision. Whether you are a local starting your morning or a traveler exploring the last frontier, our doors are open and the coffee is always fresh.

We believe coffee is more than a drink — it is a ritual, a conversation starter, and a reason to slow down in a fast-paced world.
