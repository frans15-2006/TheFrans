Who I Am

I'm a BSIT student from Palawan, Philippines, studying at Palawan Technological College, Inc. Currently serving as IT Specialist at Bureau of Internal Revenue — RDO 036, Puerto Princesa City. I build web applications with a focus on the backend — how data flows, how systems hold up, and how things work when real people are using them.

I built Mix & Match Kain for a real client, deployed it to a real restaurant, and debugged concurrent-write failures during live service without taking the system offline. That's the standard I hold work to.

Daily development runs a local 30B-parameter coding model on-device via Ollama — GPU-accelerated, no cloud dependency, no data leaving the machine.
