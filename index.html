Mix & Match Kain

Food ordering system for a real restaurant in Palawan. Customers order from their own phones via QR code. The Node.js / Express backend runs on a local machine over LAN, serving customer devices through restaurant WiFi with no internet dependency. Built to keep working through connectivity loss.

Production incidents
Duplicate order writes caused by simultaneous stock updates under peak load — fixed live without downtime.
