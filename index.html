Mix & Match Kain

A food ordering system built for a real restaurant in Palawan. Customers order from their own phones via QR code. The Node.js/Express backend runs locally on-site — deployed on a local machine over LAN with Express serving customer devices through restaurant WiFi. Designed to continue operating without internet connectivity. Resolved duplicate order writes caused by simultaneous stock updates during peak ordering. Survived a full network outage mid-event without interruption.
