What guests keep coming back for

Public listings describe a fusion of Filipino, American, and Italian plates, while guest reviews repeatedly call out the breakfast dishes, hearty mains, and drinks that make this a reliable airport-side stop.

Breakfast plates with local roots

Home-made tapa with egg and rice, plus Palawan lamayo made with rabbitfish, egg, and rice, show up in guest photos and review notes for a reason.

02

Comfort food for longer stays

Reviewers highlight fish steak, burgers, stuffed squid, pizza, wings, and fried chicken, with generous portions that suit travelers and families alike.

03

Coffee, matcha, and cold drinks

Guests mention amazing coffee, matcha, mango shakes, smoothies, mocktails, and cocktails, making the cafe side just as important as the food.

Story

A calm landing spot near the airport

MeetUp Cafe and Bar sits in Mercado de San Miguel in Puerto Princesa City, close to Puerto Princesa International Airport. Public listings describe a cozy interior, free wifi, and a menu that blends Filipino, American, and Italian flavors with coffee and bar drinks. Reviews consistently point to friendly staff, generous servings, and an easy place to settle in before a flight, after a landing, or between plans in the city.

Good food, good company, and room to breathe.
