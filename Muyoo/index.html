Our Story

Nurtured
by Nature.

Muyoo Philippines was born from a simple belief — that exceptional baked goods deserve to be shared. Inspired by Singapore's legendary mochi bun culture, we brought the recipe to Manila and made it our own.

Every bun is freshly baked daily. No day-old stock, no compromise. When they're gone, they're gone — and they always go fast. Over 50,000 people have found their way to our booths, and every single one of them comes back.

We operate as pop-ups across Metro Manila — find us at events, markets, and booths. Check our latest post for current locations.
