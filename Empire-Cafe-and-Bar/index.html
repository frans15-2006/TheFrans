About the Place

One Space.
Two Worlds.

Empire Cafe and Bar is where Tanauan, Batangas comes to begin and end the day. By morning, it's a serene cafe where coffee and all-day breakfast set the tone. By night, it transforms into a bar where craft cocktails and good company take over.

Located along President Laurel Highway, Empire is built for everyone — those who need a quiet corner to work, a table to catch up over breakfast, and a seat at the bar when the day finally winds down.
