Our Story

Good Food.
Good Mood.

Eureka started with one obsession — the California Sushi Bake. Warm, golden, loaded with flavor, and baked to perfection. Puerto Princesa hadn't tasted anything quite like it.

Now we've built a full menu around that same spirit: Buldak Omelettes, Creamy Udon, Okonomiyaki, Cloud Series drinks, Waffles — every item earned its place. No filler. No compromise.

Located at V Building, Malvar Street corner H. Mendoza, Puerto Princesa City.
