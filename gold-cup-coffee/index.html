About

Built from coffee experience and Puerto Princesa grit.

Gold Cup Coffee describes itself as the flagship brand of Gold Cup Ventures Inc., rooted in Puerto Princesa and shaped by more than a decade of coffee and hospitality experience. The brand launched in 2019 with a simple aim: make specialty coffee feel timeless, approachable, and worth slowing down for.

That goal shows up in the room as much as in the cup. Guests come for careful espresso drinks, stay for breakfast and brunch, and often end up extending the visit because the shop is open late enough to hold a second round.

"Discover a timeless experience."
