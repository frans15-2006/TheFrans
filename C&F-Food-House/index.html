🏠 About

REAL FOOD.
REAL PRICE.

C&F Food House started with one promise — great silog meals at a price everyone can afford. Every plate is ₱99. No hidden charges, no complicated orders. Just good Filipino breakfast food done right.

Located in Palanan, Makati, we're open daily from 10AM to 8PM. Whether you dine in, takeout, or order delivery — the food hits the same. Hot, filling, and worth it every time.

Weekends are when it gets serious — our Unli Silog deal keeps the tables full from open to close.
