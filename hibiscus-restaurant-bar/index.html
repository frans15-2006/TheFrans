About and chef story

A garden restaurant built around fresh, fire-warmed plates

Hibiscus Restaurant & Bar sits inside Hibiscus Garden Inn, where the dining room is framed by the same tropical, relaxed setting that the property is known for.

The restaurant page emphasizes Asian and European dishes prepared with fresh and organic ingredients, alongside firewood oven-baked pizza, salads, pasta, and seafood specials.

Public listings also describe the menu as a blend of Mediterranean and local fresh food, with oven pizza, imported meat, fresh tuna, Filipino dishes, and signature cocktails available for guests and walk-ins.

Kitchen note The line is straightforward: make the pizza wood-fired, keep the seafood fresh, and let the bar carry the evening.

"A fusion of Asian and European dishes cooked using fresh and organic ingredients are available for lunch and dinner. The fresh and delicious aroma of our wood-fired pizza will make you crave for more."
