Story

A listening room dressed as a cafe.

Vinyl Cafe Palawan is praised in public reviews for its calm courtyard feel, good espresso, greenery, and records you can actually dig through. The brand itself says it is the first cafe in Palawan with vinyl records to play, which is the energy this page follows.

The vibe is music-first, but the menu is grounded in real cafe food and drinks, from rice meals to salads, wraps, and coffee.
