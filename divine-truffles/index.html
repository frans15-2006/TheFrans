Our Story

Born from
Obsession.

Divine Truffles began with a singular obsession — creating chocolate truffles so good they felt almost otherworldly. Since July 2020, we have been crafting each piece with the kind of care that turns a simple dessert into a memorable experience.

What started as classic chocolate truffles has grown into a full collection of indulgent creations — from our iconic Dubai Chewy Cookies to the lace-thin, honey-golden world of Kataifi. Each product carries the same obsessive standard: only the finest ingredients, only the deepest flavors.

Over 85,000 people have found their way to us. We have no intention of letting any of them down.

The Kataifi Series

Golden.
Crisp. Divine.

The Kataifi Series represents the boldest expansion of the Divine Truffles universe. Inspired by the Middle Eastern tradition of kataifi — shredded phyllo pastry toasted to a honey-golden crisp — we have taken this ancient technique and filled it with flavors that span continents.

Nutella from Italy. Pistachio from the Levant. Matcha from Japan. Biscoff from Belgium. Strawberry from the garden. Each variation is a different story told through the same golden thread.
