Story / about

Comfort Food, Reworked

Grains is publicly described as plant-based comfort food by Eightynine Cafe Palawan, and it sits right on Roxas Street in Puerto Princesa City.

Recent coverage describes a simple, uncluttered room with greenery touches, friendly service, and a menu that reaches from breakfast plates to rice bowls, pasta, smoothies, juices, and acai bowls.

The result is a cafe that feels practical for locals and welcoming for travelers: easy to find, easy to settle into, and broad enough for a quick coffee stop or a full meal.

Plant-based dining in Puerto Princesa can be generous, relaxed, and genuinely satisfying.
