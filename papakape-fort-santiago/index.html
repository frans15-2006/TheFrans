Story

Coffee inside a 400-year-old cistern

Papakape Fort Santiago is housed in the Aljibe de la Fuerza, a former water cistern within the grounds of Fort Santiago. The space keeps its stone character intact while serving coffee built around Filipino flavors and familiar local desserts.

That mix of old walls and modern cups gives the branch its pull: it feels rooted in Intramuros, but the menu keeps moving forward with espresso, malunggay, black gulaman, taho-inspired coffee, and native kakanin.
