Who We Are

Built for
Palawan Roads

iGo Bikes is Puerto Princesa's go-to e-bike shop — covering everything from short tourist rentals to full unit purchases and professional e-bike servicing at our iGo Moto Garage.

Whether you're a visitor wanting to explore the city, a local looking to own your first e-bike, or a rider who needs their battery checked and motor tuned — we've got you covered.

  • Quality e-bike units — tested and road-ready
  • Trained mechanics for all e-bike brands
  • On-site charging station
  • Flexible rental rates for tourists and locals
