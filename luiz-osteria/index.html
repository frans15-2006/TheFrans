About and chef story

Visible craft, warm room

Guest-read story

Guests describe Luiz Osteria as welcoming, elegant, and spotlessly clean, with an Italian chef named Luigi praised for skill and attention. The kitchen sits close to the street, so the cooking feels part of the city rather than hidden behind it.

That makes the restaurant feel like a true osteria in the Puerto Princesa rhythm, the kind of place that works for breakfast, lunch, and dinner without losing its sense of occasion.

The kitchen is visible from the street, and guests keep calling the food a taste of Italy in the Philippines.
