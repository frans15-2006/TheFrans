Story

Built for the pause between the city and the coast

Golden Hour Cafe is known as a nature-filled roadside cafe in Bacungan, about 20 kilometers north of Puerto Princesa's city center, where cyclists, drivers, and day-trippers stop for a calm reset.

The cafe's appeal is simple: it gives the road a softer rhythm. Travelers come for the coffee, the warm plates, the mountain air, and the kind of sunset that makes a quick stop feel unhurried.

Public reviews point to a relaxed outdoor setting, friendly service, and a scene that feels especially good at dusk. It is a practical stop, but it also reads like a small reward for anyone heading north out of Puerto Princesa.
