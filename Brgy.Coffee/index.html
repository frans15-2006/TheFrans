The Idea

A coffee counter, not a chain.

Most coffee shops are built to scale. Brgy.Coffee is built to fit one street — a counter, a few stools, a menu short enough to read in one breath, and an open invitation to anyone grinding on something, whether that's a startup, a thesis, or a freelance deadline.
