Accommodations

Rooms sized for easy island time

Crown Hotel Palawan offers 82 beautifully appointed rooms. Every stay includes a flat screen TV, aircon, unlimited Wi-Fi access, a safety deposit box, a mini fridge, a writing desk and chair, coffee and tea making facilities, hot and cold shower, and complete bathroom amenities.

Amenities

Pool, dining, and event-ready spaces

From the kiddie pool and spring pool to the lap pool with a poolside bar, the hotel balances relaxed leisure with practical comforts for longer stays and special gatherings.
