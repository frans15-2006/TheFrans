About

The Coffee
Ritual.

Xin Chào Cà Phê is a Vietnamese coffee house in the heart of Puerto Princesa. We built this space around a single idea — that coffee, done right, is a slow experience. Not a transaction.

Every cup follows the same discipline: strong roast, condensed milk, the right ice, the right time. The atmosphere carries the same patience. Lantern glow, warm shadows, and a room that doesn't rush you.
