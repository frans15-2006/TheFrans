Story / About

A relaxed stop in the middle of Puerto Princesa

Reef Cafe appears across Instagram, Facebook, Tripadvisor, Restaurant Guru, and Foodpanda as a cafe-meets-bistro with friendly service, cozy interiors, and a menu that stretches from wood-fired pizza to coffee and heavier dinner plates.

Reviews consistently mention generous portions, warm service, comfortable seating, and a place people return to multiple times during a stay in Puerto Princesa. That makes the cafe feel less like a quick stop and more like a reliable local anchor on Rizal Avenue.

The public address and social profile details point to Badjao Inn on Rizal Avenue, which matches the cafe's position in the heart of the city rather than on the outskirts.
