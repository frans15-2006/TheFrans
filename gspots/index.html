Welcome

This Is Where
Adventure Begins.

Palawan has been called the last frontier of the Philippines — and for good reason. From the limestone karsts of El Nido to the crystal waters of Coron, from the longest white sand beach in the country to one of the longest navigable underground rivers in the world.

This is a place that consistently tops the world's best island lists. Conde Nast. Travel + Leisure. National Geographic. They all say the same thing: there is nowhere quite like Palawan.

We are here to show you where to go, where to eat, and where to sit and let the world slow down for a while.

Top Tourist Spots

Five Places
That Will Change You.

These are the top 5 famous spots in Palawan. Each one a world of its own. Explore and enjoy — and don't forget to look up.

01
El Nido, Palawan
El Nido
Spectacular lagoons, white sand beaches, rocky islets, towering limestone cliffs, and crystal waters full of marine life. Consistently ranked among the best islands in the world — and it earns every bit of that praise.
02
Coron, Palawan
Coron
Coral reefs, WWII shipwrecks beneath crystal-clear water, and some of the most spectacular diving in the Philippines. Above the surface, equally magnificent.
03
San Vicente, Palawan
San Vicente
Home to Long Beach — the longest white sand beach in the Philippines and the second-longest in all of Southeast Asia. Nestled between El Nido and Puerto Princesa, still untouched.
04
Puerto Princesa, Palawan
Underground River
One of the largest cave systems in the world. The longest navigable underground river on Earth. A UNESCO World Heritage Site — and one of the New Seven Wonders of Nature.
05
Puerto Princesa, Palawan
Baker's Hill
Originally a beloved bakery known for its hopia and pastries, now a gorgeous park open to the public. The perfect place to buy pasalubong and end your Palawan adventure on a sweet note.

Restaurants

Fill Your Belly.
Fill Your Heart.

After touring the stunning attractions, here are five restaurants that will complete your Palawan experience.

01
Floating Restaurant · Seafood
Badjao Seafront
A floating eatery constructed in a mangrove area with breathtaking views of Puerto Princesa's marine environment. The kind of place where the view is as good as the food — and the food is very, very good.
Abueg Street, Puerto Princesa City
02
Bar & Grill · Seafood
K'na Boyet
Fresh seafood by the Baywalk, prepared to your specifications, at reasonable prices. Bring your bargaining skills for the shrimp.
Baywalk, Puerto Princesa City
03
Filipino · Chicken
Haim Chicken Inato
The original and first chicken house in Puerto Princesa since 1999. Now one of the premier restaurants in Palawan — a true institution.
Puerto Princesa City, Palawan
04
Resort Restaurant · Coron
Balinsasayaw
Excellent service and superior facilities in Coron, Palawan. Room service, tours, family rooms, restaurant — everything you need for an unforgettable stay.
Coron, Palawan
05
Native · Exotic · Grill
Kinabuch's Grill & Bar
Native-inspired bar & grill near the Provincial Capitol. Serving Palawan's exotic food — tamilok, crocodile sisig — for the adventurous palate.
Near Provincial Capitol, Palawan

About This Site

Old But
Gold.

This site was originally built by a group of Grade 11 students from Palawan Technological College, Inc. as a school project — their first time building a website, their first time putting something on the internet and calling it theirs.

That original site, built on Weebly in 2022, is still alive to this day. It survived long after the school year ended, long after anyone expected it to still be there.

This redesign is a tribute to where it all started. Same content. Same heart. Just grown up now — the way its creator has grown up since then.

Making a website is a unique way to communicate with the rest of the world. There are no limitations on what you may do — whether you want to share your passion, educate people, or simply show what you love. If you have a website, you have a ton of alternatives.

— Original mission statement, Palawan G! Spots, 2022
