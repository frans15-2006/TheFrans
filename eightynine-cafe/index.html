Story

Warm, central, and easy to stay awhile.

EightyNine Cafe sits on Roxas Street in Puerto Princesa and reads like a true neighborhood stop: warm interiors, steady coffee, and a menu that moves comfortably from breakfast to dinner. Guests and local write-ups keep pointing to the cafe's friendly service, latte art, and comfort-leaning dishes as the reason it stays busy.

It is the kind of place that fits a slow morning, a working lunch, or a dessert run after sunset. The tone is relaxed, the flavors are familiar, and the room carries the easy rhythm of Palawan days.

Comfort food served with quality and expertly crafted coffee, right in the center of Puerto Princesa.
