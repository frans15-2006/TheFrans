Story / About

Vintage energy in the center of town

iToys Coffee Haus reads like a neighborhood classic: air-conditioned inside, garden seating at the back, and enough menu range to cover coffee, breakfast, desserts, and a full meal when you want to stay longer than planned.

That balance is what keeps the cafe relevant. It feels relaxed without feeling empty, lively without becoming rushed, and familiar whether you are stopping in for a quick cup or settling in for a long conversation.

Guests regularly mention friendly service, fair prices, and a setting that works just as well for a slow breakfast as it does for an afternoon coffee break.
