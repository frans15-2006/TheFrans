Our Story

Straight from
the Grill

Natad Bar and Grill is where Borongan comes together — bamboo walls, open skies, and the smell of charcoal in the air. Born as a neighborhood spot and grown into a landmark, we serve honest Filipino food the way it should be: grilled fresh, eaten loud, washed down cold.

From skewer to plate, everything here carries the heat of the grill and the warmth of Eastern Samar.
