Rooms / accommodations

Rooms for every pace

From solo overnights to easy shared stays, Airo keeps the essentials close and the booking choice clear.

Standard Single

Solo comfort with the essentials

Standard Single Rooms are made for solo travelers looking for comfort and affordability, with a flat-screen TV, free Wi-Fi, desk, private bathroom, toiletries, vanity basin and mirror, and room slippers.

Flat-screen TV Free Wi-Fi Desk Private bathroom

Standard Double

Made for a comfortable couple's stay

Standard Double Rooms are perfect for couples looking for a comfortable stay, with air-conditioning, toiletries, private bathroom, shower, free Wi-Fi, phone, desk, flat-screen TV, and TV.

Air-conditioning Private bathroom Shower Free Wi-Fi

Deluxe Double

More room to unwind

Deluxe Double Rooms are ideal for couples who want more space, with free Wi-Fi, desk, coat hangers, and a flat-screen TV. Additional breakfast can be arranged at Estoria Manila, G/F Anex Hotel, from 6AM to 10AM.

Free Wi-Fi Desk Coat hangers Flat-screen TV

Deluxe Twin

Practical sharing for travel companions

Deluxe Twin Rooms are perfect for sharing travelers looking for affordable accommodation, with a flat-screen TV, desk, room slippers, free Wi-Fi, shower, towels, toiletries, and hand soap. Breakfast can be arranged at Estoria Manila, G/F Anex Hotel.

Flat-screen TV Room slippers Free Wi-Fi Shower

Economy Double

Value-minded and easy to book

Economy Double Rooms are a good fit for couples looking for a more affordable stay, with non-smoking comfort, flat-screen TV, linen, desk, phone, free Wi-Fi, shower, and private bathroom.

Non-smoking Flat-screen TV Free Wi-Fi Private bathroom
