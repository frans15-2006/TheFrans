Michelin Bib Gourmand

Founded in summer 2012 by Abba Napa, Eliza Antonino, and Jon Syjuco, The Moment Group opened its first three restaurants within eight months of each other. Over a decade, they built thirteen food brands — from Manam's celebrated Filipino comfort dishes to Din Tai Fung's world-famous xiaolongbao — opening 56 doors across Metro Manila and beyond.

momentfood.com is their in-house ordering platform, backed by Mo'Go, their own delivery fleet. Every brand under one roof. Same-day delivery, same-day pickup, pre-orders up to two weeks out.
