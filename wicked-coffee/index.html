About the shop

Garage roots. Slow bar discipline.

Public coverage describes Wicked Coffee as a homegrown neighborhood cafe that started inside a garage before growing into a proper slow bar. The tone across reviews is consistent: serious coffee, a small but sharp menu, and a place built for lingering without the usual cafe noise.

Social posts from the shop read the same way, with the profile calling it a smol neighborhood coffee shop and keeping the daily rhythm tight. That attitude is what this page leans into: dark, direct, and a little wicked.
