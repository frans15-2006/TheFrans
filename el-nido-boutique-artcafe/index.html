Story / About

Built as El Nido's one-stop shop

One stop shop in El Nido for Food, Souvenirs and Travel needs.

What started as a small T-shirt shop and island tour desk grew into a cafe, boutique, and travel center under one roof. Upstairs, the restaurant looks over El Nido Bay. Downstairs, the boutique carries local souvenirs, beachwear, snacks, cameras, accessories, and practical travel services.

Freshly baked breads, live music, solar power, free purified drinking water, and bamboo straws are part of the cafe's everyday rhythm. The feeling is casual, but the intention is thoughtful: serve well, source locally where possible, and make it easy to stay a little longer.
