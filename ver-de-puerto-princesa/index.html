Story

Rooted in nature. Refined by craft.

Ver De Palawan began its journey on January 11, 2020, in Puerto Princesa, driven by a passion to elevate vegan cuisine. The team builds dishes from legumes, vegetables, grains, fruits, herbs, and spices to create something indulgent, exciting, and satisfying.

Everyone is welcome here. The space is designed to feel calm, planted, and generous, with food that aims to work for plant-based regulars and curious first-timers alike.
