About

Small Room, Big Heat

Public reviews paint Pergola Thai as a compact, personal restaurant on Rizal Avenue, with dishes cooked to order and staff who happily steer guests toward the better bowls and curries.

The Thai side of the menu gets the most love, but the house also serves European comfort food. That mix is part of the appeal: a place where pad thai can sit next to steak, pomelo salad, and a very direct green curry.

Review signal: made-to-order cooking, friendly service, and a menu that leans Thai first.
