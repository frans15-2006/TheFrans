Story

Built for a slower sip in a faster city

The visual system is intentionally spare: a dark field, amber accents, and typography that stays readable at phone distance. It keeps the focus on the brand while giving the page enough motion to feel alive.

Puerto Princesa deserves pages that feel calm under bright daylight and confident after sunset. This one treats coffee as a pause, not a dashboard.

Design language

Bebas Neue, JetBrains Mono, and Space Grotesk set a sharp hierarchy without crowding the page.

Motion language

GSAP handles section reveals, scroll-linked drift, and a quiet pulse on the primary calls to action.

Gallery

Asymmetric frame set for a dark cafe mood

The tiles are abstract by design so they can ship without missing assets, broken links, or fake photography. Each one keeps the amber accent and the same low-clutter rhythm.
